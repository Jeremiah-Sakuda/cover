## 00:00:00.000 — A bounded review, not a promised sale

Cover helps vendors purchase a defined human review from a founder. A five dollar hold reserves a twenty four hour response window. Payment depends on completing the review, never on a positive verdict. This preview uses actual local interface captures edited into a narrated walkthrough. The companies and payments are simulated; this is not a continuous screen recording or live PayPal evidence.
